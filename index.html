Portfolio Construction

The math behind the conviction.

Construction
Fund$25M · 10-yr closed-end
Fee structure2% management · 20% carry
Portfolio20–25 companies
Initial check$100K–1M · 1–5% entry
ReservesFollow-on over 2–3 rounds
Late-stageBeyond the fund → LP SPVs, deal-by-deal
Return architecture

Reserves defend ownership: we follow our winners through 2–3 rounds to limit dilution, instead of letting our stake bleed away round by round. One fund-returner is a ~$1B+ outcome — we underwrite each company to that bar.

Small initial probes, bigger follow-on into what's working — a loser costs the first check, a winner gets the reserves. When a winner outgrows the fund, LPs take the late-stage allocation — e.g. a Series D — deal-by-deal via SPVs.

Illustrative model built from the stated fund terms. Not a projection or guarantee of returns.
